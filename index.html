Frequently asked questions

Are these real downloadable apps?

The current listings are sample content to demonstrate the interface. Verified app files and download links have not been connected yet.

Can I search and filter apps?

Yes. Use the search box, category filters, and sorting menu to explore the sample listings.

Will Appistore support app submissions?

An app submission and review workflow is planned for a later version. Submissions are not enabled in this preview.

Is this website mobile friendly?

Yes. The layout adapts to phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.
